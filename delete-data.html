Delete your data

Applies to: Sabbih for Android, by DEVELOPER_NAME

Sabbih has no accounts and keeps no data on any server. All of your data, meaning your dhikr list, counts, history and settings, is stored only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

Option 1: Clear the app's data and keep the app

  • Open your phone's Settings.
  • Go to Apps and choose Sabbih.
  • Tap Storage (on some phones, Storage and cache).
  • Tap Clear data (or Clear storage) and confirm.

The app will start fresh the next time you open it.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

Press and hold the Sabbih icon, then tap Uninstall. Uninstalling removes the app and all of its data from your phone.

Device backups

If your phone's backup is turned on, Android may have saved a copy of the app's data in your device backup. You can manage or delete backups in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Backup) or in your Google account. We never have access to these backups.

What we keep

Nothing. We don't collect or store any of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
