Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

In short: Sabbih does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you record in the app stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet access.

This policy explains how the Sabbih app ("the app") handles information. The app is made by MM Tech Labs ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. We have no servers, and the app does not request permission to use the internet, so it cannot send information to us or to anyone else.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not use advertising IDs or device identifiers.

Information stored on your phone

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your list of dhikr, including any you add, and their targets
  • Your counts: the current count, lifetime totals, and daily totals used for history and streaks
  • Your settings, such as vibration preference and whether you've seen the pocket mode guide

Other apps can't read this storage, and we can't see it.

Device backups

If you have turned on backup for your Android phone, Android may include the app's stored data in your device backup (for example, in your Google account) so it can be restored on a new phone. This is a feature of Android that you control in your phone's settings. We never receive or have access to these backups.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: used to give a gentle tick for each count and a stronger buzz when a round is complete. You can turn vibration off in the app.

In pocket mode the app keeps the screen awake and lowers its brightness while you count. This needs no special permission and stops when you leave pocket mode.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or social media components, and shares no data with third parties.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data stored by the app at any time by uninstalling it, or in Android settings under Apps → Sabbih → Storage → Clear data. See Delete your data for step-by-step instructions.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app ever needs to handle data differently, we will explain it here before that version is released.
