Sabbih

A calm, simple dhikr and tasbih counter. Tap or swipe up to count, even with the screen dark in your pocket.

Count by touch

Tap anywhere or swipe up. A gentle tick for each count and a stronger buzz when a round is done.

Pocket mode

The screen goes dark but keeps counting, so you can keep your eyes and heart on your dhikr.

Your history

Lifetime totals, daily streaks, and totals for each dhikr. Add your own dhikr and targets.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. The app has no internet access, so your counts never leave your phone.
